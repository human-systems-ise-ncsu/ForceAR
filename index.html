Built by hand. Measures every push.

Here's how it comes together.

How it's made

Angle aluminum, a hacksaw, a drill and a 3D printer. Ten steps from raw stock to a working plate.

Try it

Push on the plate. Drag to move where you press, switch to Tilt to lean the push sideways, and see how the four corners share the load.

Idealized model: a rigid plate on four identical supports. Good for intuition, not for calibration.
